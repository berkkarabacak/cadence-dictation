Terms

Use Cadence kindly. Cancel when you like.

These terms cover the Cadence websites, the in-browser demo, and the native apps when they ship. They are written in plain language on purpose.

The product

Cadence turns speech into text and, on Mac, can take notes on meetings and voice memos. The browser demo on this site is a preview that uses your browser’s speech engine. Native apps type into the focused field on your machine.

Accounts and plans

Free does not need a card. Pro is billed monthly or annually per user. Enterprise is a written agreement. You can cancel Pro from Plan & billing; access continues through the period you already paid.

Acceptable use

Do not use Cadence to break the law, to impersonate someone in a way that causes harm, or to overload the service. We may suspend an account that does.

Your content

You keep the words. We need a limited license to process audio and text so the product can work — cleanup, sync, Notetaker. We do not claim copyright in your mail, your prompts, or your grocery list.

The demo is a demo

The in-browser app and the four rooms are illustrations. Accuracy depends on the browser, the mic, and the room you are in. Native apps will differ.
