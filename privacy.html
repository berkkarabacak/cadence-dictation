Privacy

Your voice is a draft, not inventory.

This page is a product note, not a courtroom filing. It describes how Cadence treats spoken audio and transcripts in the apps we ship.

We do not sell voice data

Recordings and transcripts are not a side business. We do not sell them, and we do not rent them to advertisers.

Privacy Mode

Privacy Mode is a switch: spoken audio from your account is not used to improve models. You can turn it on yourself. Enterprise teams can require it for everyone.

Notetaker stays on the Mac

Meeting notes are captured on the device. Cadence does not send a bot into the call. In-person conversations work the same way — the laptop is the listener, not a guest in the meeting list.

What we store

On Free and Pro, we may keep snippets, a personal dictionary, and meeting notes you chose to save, so the product can sync across your devices. You can delete a note, a snippet, or the account.

Questions

Write to privacy@cadence.example. If you need a DPA or a BAA conversation for a team rollout, start with hello@cadence.example.
